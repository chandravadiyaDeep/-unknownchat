A small corner of the internet for real conversations.

unknownchat pairs people who have a question with people who are willing to answer. Choose a role, wait for a match, and talk privately. There are no public chat rooms, follower counts, profiles, or message archives.

How matching works

  1. An Asker submits a question and becomes available in the temporary lobby.
  2. An Answerer joins and can be matched with one waiting Asker.
  3. The browsers negotiate a direct WebRTC connection through Trystero. Chat messages are sent to the matched peer, not broadcast to the lobby.
  4. When either person ends the chat or disconnects, the session is cleared from this page.

Community safety rules

  • Never request or share passwords, payment information, exact location, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal details.
  • Do not use this service for emergencies. Contact local emergency services or someone you trust if you are in danger.

  • Do not assume that a stranger's claims or advice are accurate. Use your judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration, username, profile, or password.

Are messages stored?

unknownchat has no application database and does not intentionally save chat messages. Messages are held in browser memory during a session and cleared when the session ends or the page is closed. Your browser, operating system, network provider, or the other participant may still retain information outside this site's control.

Is this completely anonymous?

No internet service can promise absolute anonymity. WebRTC peers may learn network information such as IP addresses, and public signaling infrastructure may process connection metadata. Do not share information that could identify you.

Can someone see my conversation in the lobby?

The app sends chat messages only to the selected peer through a targeted Trystero action. The lobby uses temporary presence and matching messages. This is not a guarantee against a modified client or a compromised device.

Why can't I find anyone?

There may be no one currently waiting in the opposite role. Connection discovery also depends on public signaling relays and your network allowing WebRTC connections. Keep the page open or try again later.

Does unknownchat provide professional advice?

No. Answers are opinions from strangers, not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

Privacy notice

What this page handles

Your selected role, question, temporary peer identifiers, connection status, and messages are held in volatile page memory to make matching and chat work. The app does not create accounts, use an application database, or intentionally persist conversation content.

Trystero uses third-party signaling relays to discover peers and exchange connection setup data. Your browser also contacts the static hosting provider and may load fonts from Google Fonts.
